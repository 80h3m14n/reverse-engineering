### Why read books??

1. Depth & Foundational Theory: Books provide structured, deep dives into the why and how of reverse engineering, which is essential when designing agentic fraud control tests.
2. Tool Agnosticism: While tools like IDA Pro, Ghidra, and Radare2 evolve rapidly, the underlying principles of binary analysis, packer detection, and control flow reconstruction remain stable.
3. Systematic Methodology: When simulating fraud, you need a repeatable methodology. Books offer proven workflows for static analysis, dynamic debugging, and fuzzing that are harder to extract from ad-hoc tutorials.
